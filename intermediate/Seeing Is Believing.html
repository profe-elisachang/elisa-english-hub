💼 Useful Expressions for Professionals

Is this a bid to...?
¿Es esto un intento de...?
Is this a bid to reduce workplace screen time and improve ergonomics?
It's by design
Es intencionalmente diseñado así
The privacy features are by design to protect employee data during facility inspections.
Take my word for it
Créeme / Confía en mí
Take my word for it—this safety protocol will prevent future incidents.
Get used to
Acostumbrarse a
Workers will need to get used to the new PPE requirements gradually.
Come with / Feature
Venir con / Incluir como característica
The new inspection tablets come with enhanced data encryption features.

👓 Meta Ray-Ban Display: AI-Powered Smart Glasses for the Workplace

ISA SOARES, CNN ANCHOR

Meta, the owners of Facebook and Instagram, is continuing to try and innovate using AI—this time, introducing a new set of smart glasses in a partnership with Ray-Ban. The new model, named Display, is a bid to ditch the smartphone and push users to interact more with their surroundings instead. Our Clare Duffy was one of the first to try them out. Have a look.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 1

owners

People or companies that possess something

dueños / propietarios

The factory owners invested in new safety equipment after the audit.
continuing

To keep doing something without stopping

continuar / seguir

The IT team is continuing to upgrade the network infrastructure this quarter.
innovate

To introduce new ideas, methods, or products

innovar

Our EHS department must innovate safety training using VR technology.
partnership

A cooperative relationship between organizations

asociación / colaboración

We formed a partnership with local universities to recruit IT talent.
bid

An attempt or effort to achieve something

intento / oferta

This new policy is a bid to reduce workplace injuries by 30%.
ditch

To get rid of or stop using something

dejar / abandonar / deshacerse de

We decided to ditch the old incident reporting system and switch to a mobile app.
interact

To communicate or work together with someone or something

interactuar

Technicians can interact with machinery using voice commands through the smart glasses.
surroundings

The environment or area around you

entorno / alrededores

Safety inspectors must stay aware of their surroundings at all times in the plant.

CLARE DUFFY, CNN CORRESPONDENT

These are the new Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, the next generation of AI-powered wearables. Meta is making a bet that with these glasses, users won't need to spend so much time looking down at their phone. Unlike previous versions, which you could only interact with via voice and audio, these feature a tiny display inside the lens, and they come with what's called a Neural Band wristband, so you can navigate simply with hand and finger gestures.

So, what I'm seeing right now on this little display—it looks like, you know, the home page of Spotify for whatever song that you're currently listening to. So, you've got the play button and the forward and backward buttons. You can shuffle. Yeah. And then, when I do the volume gesture, I see the little volume button open up. Cool.

💬 Discussion Questions

  • How could wearable technology like smart glasses improve safety inspections or equipment maintenance in our facilities? What specific tasks would benefit most?
  • What are the main privacy and data security concerns your department would need to address before implementing AI-powered wearables in the workplace?
  • The article mentions Google Glass "flopped" because it was expensive and unfashionable. What factors determine whether new technology gets adopted by workers in your experience? How do we overcome resistance to change?
  • If you could design the ideal wearable device for your daily work tasks, what features would it include? What problems would it solve that current tools don't?
  • How do you balance the benefits of new technology (like hands-free operation) against potential risks (like distraction or dependence on devices)? Can you share an example from your department?
