🧛 Vampire Routing — The Key to Cooler Urban Walking

As climate change and urbanization push city temperatures higher, walking outdoors is becoming increasingly uncomfortable in many parts of the world, especially when prolonged time spent in sunlight is involved. As a result, outdoor lovers are now seeking cooler, shaded walking routes. However, most mainstream map apps still do not clearly indicate shaded footpaths.

Enter routing.osm.ch — a Swiss web-based route planner that introduces a novel solution to this problem. Its "Vampire mode," named after the legendary creatures' fear of being exposed to natural light, was developed by a Swiss research team led by Professor Stefan Keller. The platform combines open data from OpenStreetMap with high-resolution data from the Swiss Federal Office of Topography to precisely calculate where shadows fall throughout the day.

Officially called "Foot (vampire)" mode, the feature uses shade-aware routing to suggest paths that remain in the shade. It directs people along shaded sidewalks, under tree cover, or beside buildings that block the sun. Currently available in Zurich, the mode enables people to enjoy cooler and more comfortable walks during hot summer days.

The benefits are evident. For both locals and tourists, vampire routing transforms hot summer walks into pleasant shaded strolls. Furthermore, for city planners, it offers a tool to help reduce urban heat island effects by easing heat stress in dense urban areas. Finally, vampire routing makes cities easier to walk in and therefore promotes quality of life.

🔗 Part B — Discourse Markers (conectores de texto)

Learn these as complete fixed phrases. They connect ideas in formal and professional English.

Discourse Marker En español Function In the article…
As a result, Como resultado, Shows cause → effect "As a result, people seek shaded routes."
However, Sin embargo, Introduces a contrast "However, most apps don't show shaded paths."
Furthermore, Además, Adds another supporting point "Furthermore, it helps city planners."
Finally, Por último / Finalmente, Closes a list of points "Finally, it promotes quality of life."
