1960s
"Question everything. Change the world." — The decade of revolution, color, and counterculture.
🎵
Music
  • The Beatles, The Rolling Stones
  • Motown (Stevie Wonder, Diana Ross)
  • Folk music (Bob Dylan)
  • Psychedelic rock
  • Woodstock festival (1969)
👗
Fashion
  • Mini skirts
  • Go-go boots
  • Bell-bottom jeans (late 60s)
  • Tie-dye shirts
  • Mod fashion (geometric patterns)
  • Hippie style: headbands, fringe
📺
Tech & Media
  • Black & white TV (then color)
  • Apollo 11 moon landing (1969)
  • Rotary telephone
  • Polaroid cameras
  • First video game prototypes
🌍
Big Events
  • Civil Rights Movement (MLK)
  • Vietnam War protests
  • Space Race (US vs USSR)
  • Kennedy assassination (1963)
  • First man on the moon
😎
Vibe
  • Optimism + rebellion
  • "Peace and love"
  • Question authority
  • Youth culture rising
  • Bright, bold colors everywhere

1970s
"Disco, denim, and drama." — The decade of self-expression, economic chaos, and funky good times.
🎵
Music
  • Disco (ABBA, Bee Gees, Donna Summer)
  • Funk (James Brown)
  • Rock (Led Zeppelin, Queen, Fleetwood Mac)
  • Punk rock (late 70s — Sex Pistols)
  • 8-track tapes
👗
Fashion
  • Bell-bottom jeans (very wide)
  • Platform shoes
  • Corduroy everything
  • Halter tops, wrap dresses
  • Polyester suits
  • Big hair, long sideburns
📺
Tech & Media
  • VHS / Betamax video tapes
  • First personal computers (Apple I)
  • Pong — first popular video game
  • Disco balls & nightclubs
  • Color TV becomes mainstream
🌍
Big Events
  • Oil crisis (1973) — gas shortages
  • Watergate scandal (Nixon resigns)
  • End of Vietnam War (1975)
  • Women's liberation movement grows
  • Star Wars released (1977)
😎
Vibe
  • Carefree, live for tonight
  • Groovy, laid-back
  • More individual freedom
  • Economic anxiety underneath
  • The birth of cool

1980s
"Bigger, louder, brighter — excess was the point." — Neon lights, power suits, and the birth of pop culture as we know it.
🎵
Music
  • MTV launches (1981) — music videos
  • Michael Jackson — Thriller
  • Madonna, Prince, David Bowie
  • Hair metal (Bon Jovi, Guns N' Roses)
  • Electronic / synth-pop (Depeche Mode)
  • Walkman — music everywhere
👗
Fashion
  • Shoulder pads (power dressing)
  • Acid-wash denim
  • Neon colors
  • Leg warmers, leotards
  • Permed / big hair
  • Ray-Ban Wayfarers
  • Nike Air Jordan sneakers
📺
Tech & Media
  • Walkman (Sony)
  • VHS — home movies
  • Arcade games peak (Pac-Man, Tetris)
  • Nintendo Entertainment System
  • First mobile phones (brick phones)
  • Fax machines in offices
🌍
Big Events
  • Fall of the Berlin Wall (1989)
  • AIDS epidemic begins
  • Chernobyl disaster (1986)
  • Cold War tensions peak
  • Live Aid concert (1985)
😎
Vibe
  • Greed is good
  • Show off your success
  • Work hard, play hard
  • Loud, confident, unapologetic
  • Pop culture explosion

1990s
"Whatever." — The decade of irony, grunge, and the internet being born.
🎵
Music
  • Grunge (Nirvana, Pearl Jam)
  • Boy bands (Backstreet Boys, NSYNC)
  • Britney Spears, Spice Girls
  • Hip-hop goes mainstream (Tupac, Biggie)
  • CD players / Discman
  • Mixtapes still a thing
👗
Fashion
  • Baggy jeans, flannel shirts
  • Grunge: ripped jeans, Doc Martens
  • Scrunchies, butterfly clips
  • Windbreaker jackets
  • Cargo pants
  • Platform sneakers (Spice Girls)
📺
Tech & Media
  • The internet for everyone (dial-up)
  • AOL instant messenger
  • Nokia 3310 (indestructible phone)
  • Tamagotchi
  • PlayStation 1
  • DVD replaces VHS
🌍
Big Events
  • End of the Cold War
  • Nelson Mandela freed (1990)
  • NAFTA trade agreement (1994)
  • First Gulf War (1991)
  • Y2K panic begins (late 90s)
😎
Vibe
  • Cool = not caring too much
  • Anti-establishment, ironic
  • Optimism about technology
  • Simpler, slower pace
  • "Real" and authentic

Y2K (2000–2005)
"The future is now — and it's shiny." — Optimism, tech bubbles, low-rise everything, and the rise of celebrity culture.
🎵
Music
  • Pop-punk (Blink-182, Sum 41)
  • R&B / neo-soul (Beyoncé, Alicia Keys)
  • Eminem, Jay-Z dominate hip-hop
  • iPod + iTunes changes music forever
  • Ringtones as status symbols
  • Music downloads (Napster)
👗
Fashion
  • Low-rise jeans (very low!)
  • Tiny sunglasses
  • Velour tracksuits (Juicy Couture)
  • Trucker hats, Von Dutch
  • Butterfly clips, frosted tips
  • Metallic, holographic fabrics
  • Flip phones as accessories
📺
Tech & Media
  • iPod (2001) — 1,000 songs in your pocket
  • Motorola Razr (iconic flip phone)
  • MSN Messenger, AIM
  • MySpace (first social network)
  • Digital cameras replacing film
  • PlayStation 2, Xbox
🌍
Big Events
  • Y2K bug panic → nothing happens
  • 9/11 attacks (2001)
  • Dot-com bubble bursts
  • Iraq War begins (2003)
  • Reality TV explosion (Survivor, Idol)
😎
Vibe
  • Anything is possible (tech optimism)
  • Celebrity obsession
  • Futuristic but also carefree
  • Shiny, plastic, maximalist
  • The world felt smaller (internet)
