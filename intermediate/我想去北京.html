课文一 我想去北京（一）

课文正文
我是在 1997 年喜欢上汉语、开始学习汉语的。那年，我刚考上大学dà xué，学校里新开了中文系xì，每个学生都可以学习汉语。虽然我知道汉字对我们外国人来说很难nán，而且写汉字就像画画huà huà一样，但我觉得非常很有意思yì si，决定jué dìng先学几个月试一试shì yi shì。
Vocabulary
画画 huà huà v.
To draw; to paint
Wǒ xǐhuān zài xīngqītiān huà huà.
I like to paint on Sundays.
决定 jué dìng v.
To decide
Wǒ juédìng xīngqīyī qù Běijīng.
I decided to go to Beijing on Monday.
试一试 shì yi shì v.
To give it a try; to test
Zhè kǒu hěn hǎochī, nǐ shì yi shì.
This tastes great, give it a try!
Translation I fell in love with Chinese and started learning it in 1997. That year, I was just admitted to college, and the school opened a new Chinese department...

课文正文
中文系有从中国来的老师，也有我们巴基斯坦bā jī sī tǎn的老师，中国老师大多来自北京，而ér我们国家的老师呢，也都是从北京的大学毕业的。不管bù guǎn是在课上，还是hái shi课下，他们经常谈到tán dào北京。
Vocabulary
巴基斯坦 bā jī sī tǎn n.
Pakistan
而 ér conj.
Whereas; on the other hand
Tā xǐhuān chá, ér wǒ xǐhuān kāfēi.
He likes tea, whereas I prefer coffee.
不管...还是... bù guǎn... hái shi... conj.
No matter whether... or...
谈到 tán dào v.
To talk about; to mention
Translation The Chinese department had teachers from China as well as from Pakistan. Most Chinese teachers came from Beijing, while our local teachers also graduated from Beijing universities...

课文正文
从他们的谈话tán huà中，我渐渐jiàn jiàn了解liǎo jiě了北京，知道北京很漂亮，文化很丰富fēng fù，有很多名胜古迹míng shèng gǔ jì，等等。
Vocabulary
谈话 tán huà n.
Conversation; talk
渐渐 jiàn jiàn adv.
Gradually; step by step
Tiānqì jiànjiàn biàn lěng le.
The weather is gradually turning cold.
了解 liǎo jiě v.
To understand; to learn about
丰富 fēng fù adj.
Rich; abundant
Translation From their conversations, I gradually came to know Beijing, learning that Beijing is very beautiful, rich in culture, and has many places of historical interest...

课文正文
我们的课本上也有一些谈到北京的课文，我对那些名胜古迹míng shèng gǔ jì的照片特别感兴趣gǎn xìng qù。就这样，我喜欢上了北京，爱上了北京。我很希望能到北京去，去看看故宫gù gōng、长城cháng chéng、颐和园yí hé yuán。
Vocabulary
名胜古迹 míng shèng gǔ jì n.
Places of historic interest and scenic beauty
感兴趣 gǎn xìng qù v.
To be interested in
Wǒ duì Zhōngguó wénhuà hěn gǎn xìngqù.
I am very interested in Chinese culture.
故宫 / 长城 / 颐和园 n.
The Forbidden City / The Great Wall / The Summer Palace
Translation Our textbooks also had some lessons about Beijing. I was particularly interested in photos of historical sites. That's how I fell in love with Beijing and really hope to visit...
