⚖️ Should You Always Do the Right Thing?
How important is personal happiness?
by Ruth Devlin and Linda Ludwig · Page Design by Angela Chang

No matter who you are, you likely struggle with the question of whether doing the right thing should always come before your own happiness.

On one hand, making good moral choices earns the trust and respect of others. It also gives you a clear conscience. An example is telling the truth when you'd rather avoid getting in trouble. That may be difficult in the moment, but it often prevents bigger problems later. Also, standing up for someone who is being bullied might not be comfortable, but it helps protect others and shows courage. Living with integrity usually makes you feel better than chasing short-term pleasure.

On the other hand, personal happiness is also important. If people always sacrifice their own well-being for what they believe is "right," they hurt themselves. They can end up exhausted and angry. For instance, always putting others first could prevent someone from pursuing their own dreams. Another example is spending money on a much-needed vacation instead of helping a struggling family member. Happiness and self-care are not selfish; they are necessary for a balanced life.

💡 Usage Tip

① prevent + O + from V-ing

Use this structure to say that someone or something stops another person from doing an action.

Usa esta estructura para decir que alguien o algo impide que otra persona haga una acción.

Similar verbs (each with slightly different strength):

  • keep sb from V-ing — neutral tone / tono neutro
  • stop sb from V-ing — stronger / más directo
  • discourage sb from V-ing — gentle / más suave, disuadir

📌 Always putting others first could prevent someone from pursuing their own dreams.
Poner siempre a los demás primero podría impedir que alguien persiga sus propios sueños.

② end up + V-ing  /  end up + Adj. or N.

Use end up to describe a final result — often unexpected or undesired — after a series of events.

Usa end up para describir un resultado final — a menudo inesperado o no deseado — después de una serie de eventos.

Structure Example Spanish
end up + V-ing I meant to leave early, but I ended up staying until midnight. Terminé quedándome hasta medianoche.
end up + Adj. They can end up exhausted and angry. Pueden terminar agotados y enojados.
end up + N. If he doesn't study, he'll end up a failure. Si no estudia, terminará siendo un fracaso.

⚠️ end up + Adj. → NO need for "being"!
✅ end up exhausted  |  ❌ end up being exhausted (grammatically possible but unnatural)
Con adjetivos, NO se necesita "being" — suena más natural sin él.

🤝 Conversation — Balance is the Key
Aiden: Can you give me some advice? I'm really on the fence.
Sofia: I can try. What's going on?
Aiden: My mom wants me to assist my grandma with cleaning out her house on Saturday. She's lived there for 50 years, and it's crammed full of stuff, so it'll be a big job. Problem is, there's a concert on Saturday night that I really want to go to.
Sofia: That's tough. Of course you want to help your grandma.
Aiden: I do. But I've worked hard for weeks without a break, and I need one before I have a breakdown.

Sofia: What if you help your grandma during the day and then go to the concert at night?
Aiden: I'd be too exhausted to enjoy it. And my grandma's schedule isn't flexible. She's sold her house, and everything has to be moved out by Saturday evening.
Sofia: Well, Aiden, balance is the key — helping when you can but also taking care of your own needs.
Aiden: I know. But I'll feel really guilty if I don't do the "right" thing. Still, I need to take care of myself, or I will have that breakdown! 🙈

✍️ Self-Practice Questions
1. What does "struggle with" mean? Use it in a sentence about your own life. ＋
"Struggle with" means to have difficulty with something or to find it hard to deal with.

Example answer: "I struggle with speaking English on the phone because I can't see the other person's face."

Your turn: Think of something at work or at home that is hard for you. Try: "I struggle with _____ because _____."
2. According to the article, what are TWO reasons why doing the right thing is good? ＋
According to the article:

2. It also gives you a clear conscience (you feel good inside).

Bonus: The article also says that standing up for someone who is being bullied shows courage and helps protect others.
3. In the dialog, why can't Aiden go to the concert AND help his grandma? ＋
Aiden says he would be too exhausted to enjoy the concert if he worked all day. Also, his grandma's schedule is not flexible — she has already sold her house, and everything must be moved out by Saturday evening. So there is no time to do both activities.

Sample answer: "I agree because if you don't take care of yourself, you will end up exhausted and you won't be able to help anyone. For example, if I don't sleep enough, I make more mistakes at work. So self-care helps me perform better and be a better teammate."
