🏜️ A Hotel Made of Modules

A new desert hotel feels like camping on Mars

Based on an article by Deborah Netburn, Los Angeles Times (simplified version)

In the desert near Joshua Tree National Park, there is a new hotel called Reset. It is very different from a normal hotel.

The hotel has 65 small rooms. Each room looks like a box. The boxes have silver, wavy sides. Many people think the rooms look like something from a space movie.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 1-2

wavy

Having curves, like small waves

ondulado

The metal roof of the workshop has a wavy pattern for extra strength.

The rooms are not really shipping containers. But the designers wanted the hotel to feel like a small community on Mars. They only put in things that guests really need: a bed, a coffee maker, a lantern, and a cushion for watching the stars.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 3

community

A group of people living or working together in one place

comunidad

The factory workers formed a small community around the plant.
cushion

A soft object used for sitting or resting comfortably

cojín

There is a cushion on the chair in the break room.

An architect named Ben Uyeda designed the hotel. He built the room modules in a factory in Canada. Workers built the walls and furniture there. Then they moved the modules to the desert. At the same time, other workers built the foundation in California.

Building modules in a factory is not cheaper than building a normal hotel. But it is faster. This is very useful in a place far from the city, like the desert.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 4-5

module

A separate part that can be joined with other parts to build something bigger

módulo

Each module is built in the factory before it is sent to the customer.
foundation

The base under a building that holds it up

cimientos

Workers finished the foundation before the new warehouse walls arrived.

Uyeda had another reason to build this way. In the last few years, house prices near Joshua Tree went up a lot. Many local people cannot afford to rent a house because owners rent out their houses to tourists on Airbnb instead.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 6

afford

To have enough money to pay for something

poder pagar, costear

The company could not afford new machines this year, so they repaired the old ones.
rent out

To let someone use your property in exchange for money

alquilar, arrendar

The company rents out its extra warehouse space to smaller businesses.

Uyeda thought that modular hotels could help. If companies build hotels on land that is already zoned for business, they do not need to use houses that local families need.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 7

zoned for

Officially marked for a specific type of use, like business or housing

zonificado para

This land is zoned for factories, not for houses.

Even so, staying at Reset is expensive. A room can cost around $200 a night. Some tourists think it is worth it. Others think the money could help build more affordable housing instead.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 8

worth it

Good enough to justify the money, time, or effort spent

que vale la pena

The training was expensive, but it was worth it for the team.
affordable

Priced at a level that people can reasonably pay

asequible, accesible

The company looked for a more affordable supplier for the new parts.

A journalist who visited the hotel said the rooms felt very plain at first. But after a few days, the simple design started to feel special to her. She liked looking up at the sky from her private patio every night.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 9

plain

Simple, with no decoration or extra details

sencillo, sin decoración

The new packaging design is plain, with no bright colors or logos.

💬 Discussion Questions

  • Tourism vs. Housing Crisis: Airbnb lets owners rent houses to tourists instead of local families. Do you think this is fair? What should cities do about it?
  • Modular Construction in Mexico: Building rooms in a factory and moving them to the site is common in some countries. Do you think this could work well in Mexico? Why or why not?
  • Would You Stay Here?: Would you pay $200 a night to stay in a small, simple room like this? What would make it worth the price for you?
  • Only What You Need: The hotel only has things guests really need — nothing extra. Could your workplace use this same idea? What could be removed?

Reference Answers
  1. a) module   b) foundation   c) afford   d) affordable
  2. a) True   b) False (it costs about $200 a night)   c) True   d) False (the rooms are plain and simple)
  3. a) Because the modules are built in a factory at the same time the foundation is built at the hotel site, which saves time.   b) Because owners rent their houses to tourists on Airbnb instead of to local families.
