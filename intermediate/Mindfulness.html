🧘 Mindfulness: Failing to Make the Grade in Schools

Mindfulness—the practice of paying deliberate attention to the present moment—has surged in popularity in recent years. Advocates claim it greatly reduces stress and improves mental health, and schools across the US have added mindfulness lessons to their class schedules. However, despite its appeal, the practice may not be as beneficial to students as it's believed to be.

📖 Vocabulary - Paragraph 1

deliberate

Done with careful thought and intention

Deliberado, intencional

"She made a deliberate choice to focus on quality."

surged

Increased suddenly and powerfully

Aumentó repentinamente

"Demand for electric vehicles surged last year."

advocates

People who publicly support or recommend something

Defensores, promotores

"Environmental advocates pushed for stricter regulations."

beneficial

Producing good or helpful results

Beneficioso, provechoso

"Regular exercise is beneficial for your health."

Several large studies from the UK, including one of 28,000 students across 100 schools, had mixed or negative results. For some students, being asked to meditate in the presence of their classmates actually increased anxiety. For others, focusing on their feelings proved upsetting rather than calming. Each student, after all, is different, so forcing mindfulness on entire classes in a one-size-fits-all way may simply be too blunt an approach.

📖 Vocabulary - Paragraph 2

meditate

To focus one's mind for relaxation or spiritual purposes

Meditar

"I meditate for 10 minutes every morning."

upsetting

Making someone feel worried, unhappy, or disturbed

Perturbador, molesto

"The news was very upsetting to the team."

forcing

Making someone do something against their will

Forzar, obligar

"We shouldn't be forcing people to work overtime."

blunt

Direct and lacking subtlety; not sharp or refined

Directo, franco, sin sutileza

"Using a blunt approach might offend some customers."

Advocates of teaching mindfulness in schools often quote reports of improved focus and reduced conflict. But studies show that these benefits, if present at all, often fade quickly. Indeed, without professional guidance and one-on-one support, teaching mindfulness in schools risks doing more harm than good. Simply put, it could cause schools to neglect deeper issues while diverting resources from proven solutions.

📖 Vocabulary - Paragraph 3

To gradually disappear or lose strength

Desvanecerse, desaparecer

"The color will fade after several washes."

neglect

To fail to care for or give attention to something

Descuidar, ignorar

"Don't neglect your personal development."

diverting

Redirecting something to a different course or purpose

Desviar, redirigir

"They are diverting funds from marketing to R&D."

✍️ Self-Practice Questions

1. What does "one-size-fits-all" mean? Can you give an example from your workplace?

Sample Answer: "One-size-fits-all" means a single solution applied to everyone without considering individual differences. In my workplace, we had a one-size-fits-all training program that didn't work well because team members had different skill levels and learning styles.

2. Describe a situation where something "surged" in your industry or daily life.

Sample Answer: During the pandemic, online shopping surged dramatically. Our logistics team had to work overtime because the number of orders increased by 300% in just two months. We had to hire temporary staff and extend warehouse hours.

3. When might a "blunt" approach be appropriate in business communication?

Sample Answer: A blunt approach might be appropriate in urgent situations or when clarity is critical. For example, if there's a safety issue in the warehouse, we need to be blunt and direct to prevent accidents. However, in most situations, it's better to be tactful.

4. Have you ever seen a new policy or initiative "fade quickly" at work? What happened?

Sample Answer: Yes, our company introduced a new quality control checklist, but enthusiasm faded quickly after the first month. People returned to their old habits because there was no follow-up training or supervision. The initiative needed better support and accountability.
