📖 Article

Candles were made from animal fats in ancient times. As knowledge and materials improved, people began making them with beeswax and vegetable wax, which burned more cleanly and produced less odor. Before lighting, candles were considered highly valuable. In many religions, they came to symbolize life and wisdom. Even today, candles are frequently used in religious rituals and for cultural celebrations.

The shift toward industrial candle making began in 1834, when Joseph Morgan patented a machine that could produce molded candles without interruption. His invention marked the start of mechanized production, though for many decades, candles were still made mainly in small workshops. Since the early 20th century, however, candle making has grown into a large-scale industry. The use of synthetic oils and modern scent-blending techniques has made it possible to mass-produce a wide range of scented items that were once crafted only by hand.

Scented candles today can be made from countless combinations of fragrances. Some candles are designed to evoke seasonal moods or natural environments, such as fresh spring rain or a misty mountain forest. Others capture the distinctive scents associated with major holidays, like Thanksgiving or Christmas.

Typically, scented candles release fragrance in three phases: top notes, heart notes, and base notes. When a candle is first lit, the top notes are the most prominent. After about an hour, the heart notes become apparent, and as the candle continues to burn, the base notes slowly emerge.

A well-chosen scent can make any space feel more inviting and even affect one's mood. Some scents help us feel energetic and are perfect for lively gatherings. Others can offer a feeling of calm for a relaxing evening. By stimulating our sense of smell, scented candles are effective tools for creating a comfortable atmosphere. They can also provide therapeutic benefits, helping lift spirits, relieve stress, or promote restful sleep. No wonder they continue to be cherished in homes around the world.
