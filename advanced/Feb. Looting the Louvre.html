✍️ Self-Practice Questions

1. Fill in the blank:

The thieves __________ the museum using a truck lift and escaped on motorcycles.

2. True or False:

Most museum heists take between 30 minutes to an hour to complete.

3. Vocabulary matching:

Match the word with its meaning:

  • a) disassemble - i) to understand and share feelings
  • b) sympathize - ii) to take apart into pieces
  • c) catastrophic - iii) extremely harmful

4. Short answer:

Why does Anthony Amore say that destroying the cultural pieces would be more concerning than simply stealing them intact?

Reference Answers
  1. broke into (The thieves broke into the museum using a truck lift and escaped on motorcycles.)
  2. False - According to the expert, most museum heists occur between 3 and 9 minutes.
  3. a-ii, b-i, c-iii
  4. Because if thieves disassemble the pieces (like removing jewels from a tiara), the historical and cultural integrity is permanently destroyed. While the monetary value might be realized through selling individual parts, the irreplaceable cultural significance would be lost forever.
