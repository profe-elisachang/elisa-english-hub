✍️ Self-Practice
📌 What does “go public” mean, and how is a direct listing different from an IPO?
“Go public” means a company lists its shares on a public exchange. A direct listing (like Spotify’s) doesn’t issue new shares or use underwriters; it simply lets existing shareholders sell shares directly, saving on bank fees. In an IPO, the company raises new capital by issuing new shares with the help of investment banks.
📌 Explain the phrase “feed into” using an example from the article.
In the article, Spotify alumni “feed experience into” the Swedish tech ecosystem — meaning they contribute their knowledge and skills to help new startups grow. For example, former Spotify employees have launched over 120 investment-backed startups, feeding their expertise into the broader tech community.
📌 What is a “freemium funnel”? How does Spotify use it?
A freemium funnel is a model where users start with a free version and some convert to paid subscriptions. Spotify uses it by offering free access with ads, then enticing users with premium features like offline listening, no ads, and higher audio quality. The “top of the funnel” refers to the large number of new users entering the free tier.
📌 Why is “listening age” a clever marketing feature for Spotify Wrapped?
It adds a playful, personality‑driven element that makes the summary highly shareable, sparking conversations and reinforcing user identity — which boosts both acquisition (new users see it and sign up) and retention (existing users look forward to it every year). It also taps into people's curiosity about how their music taste compares to others.
📌 What does Gustav mean when he says Wrapped “dominates the cultural conversation”?
He means that for about a week in December, everyone — online and offline — is talking about their Wrapped results. It becomes a shared social experience, discussed at work, on social media, and among friends, much like a major cultural event.
