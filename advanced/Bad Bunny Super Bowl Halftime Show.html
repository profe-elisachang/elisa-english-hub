🏈 GOP Lawmaker Calls for Probe Into NFL and NBC Over 'Indecent' Bad Bunny Super Bowl Halftime Show

by Chad de Guzman · Reporter · TIME

 💬  Useful Expressions

call for a probe into...
exigir una investigación sobre...
The senator called for a probe into the company's safety record after the accident.

Most people complained online, but she took her concerns to another level by filing a formal report.
reach out to (someone) for comment
contactar a alguien para obtener comentarios

Anticipating the controversy, the organizers prepared a detailed press statement in advance.

📰 Article: Paragraph 1

Bad Bunny's Super Bowl halftime show was divisive before it even started, with many conservatives criticizing the Puerto Rican singer's political outspokenness, including condemning Immigration and Customs Enforcement last week at the Grammy Awards, where he took home the prize for album of the year. But while many fans lauded the Latin hitmaker's 13-minute set on Sunday, despite President Donald Trump calling it "absolutely terrible" and "an affront to the Greatness of America," one Republican critic is taking his offense to another level.

📰 Article: Paragraph 2

Rep. Andy Ogles (R, Tenn.) posted on X on Monday evening a letter addressed to the House Energy and Commerce Committee in which he called for an immediate inquiry into the National Football League and broadcaster NBCUniversal over their "prior knowledge, review, and approval" of what he alleged to be "a performance dominated by sexually explicit lyrical themes and suggestive choreography." In his post, Ogles called the halftime show "pure smut" and claimed that it featured "gyrating provocatively" and Bad Bunny "shamelessly" performing explicit moves.

📰 Article: Paragraph 3

The Tennessee lawmaker, whom Trump has previously called a "Conservative Warrior," argued in his letter that songs in Bad Bunny's set included references to sexual content that would be "readily apparent across any language barrier." While Bad Bunny did perform parts of his song "Safaera," he refrained from singing the more explicit parts of those lyrics during the halftime show. Ogles argued that it was "highly implausible" that the NFL and NBC lacked advance knowledge of the set's content. He requested that the committee examine the extent of executives' knowledge, the internal review and translation processes, and whether safeguards—such as broadcast delay protocols—were "properly applied" or "intentionally disregarded."

📰 Article: Paragraph 4

"These flagrant, indecent acts are illegal to be displayed on public airways," Ogles declared. "American culture will not be mocked or corrupted without consequence." TIME reached out to the NFL and NBC for comment. In a previous X post, Ogles said that Bad Bunny's performance was "conclusive proof" that Puerto Rico should never be a state. Another GOP congressman, Rep. Randy Fine (R, Fla.), asserted on X earlier Monday that the halftime show was "illegal," attaching screenshots of the translated lyrics. Fine said he would send Federal Communications Commission chairperson Brendan Carr—who last year pressured ABC to suspend late night comedian Jimmy Kimmel—a letter to call for "dramatic action," including potentially fines and broadcast license reviews, against the NFL, NBC, and Bad Bunny.

📰 Article: Paragraph 5

Anticipating the controversy over Bad Bunny's performance, conservative activist group Turning Point USA staged counterprogramming advertised as a celebration of "faith, family, and freedom" to cater to the MAGA crowd. The group's "All-American Halftime Show" was headlined by Trump-supporting musician Kid Rock—known for songs such as "Cool, Daddy Cool," which he didn't perform during the Turning Point USA halftime show, as well as "Bawitdaba," which he did perform, including a verse that shouts out "topless dancers" and "hookers" in Hollywood.

✏️: Self-Practice Questions

1. Fill in the blank: Bad Bunny ______________ from singing the more explicit parts of his lyrics during the halftime show. (Use the correct form of the verb that means "to deliberately avoid doing something.")

2. True or False: Rep. Andy Ogles claimed that the NFL and NBC definitely did not know about the content of Bad Bunny's performance before the show.

3. Matching — Match the word to its meaning:

a) divisive    b) safeguards    c) flagrant    d) counterprogramming

1. Done openly and shockingly without shame
2. Measures put in place to prevent harm or violations
3. Scheduling a rival event at the same time as a popular show
4. Causing strong disagreement between groups of people

4. Short answer: In your own words, what did Rep. Randy Fine threaten to do after watching Bad Bunny's halftime show?

Reference Answers
  1. Bad Bunny refrained from singing the more explicit parts of his lyrics during the halftime show.
  2. False. Ogles argued it was "highly implausible" that the NFL and NBC lacked advance knowledge — meaning he believed they did know about the content beforehand.
  3. a–4, b–2, c–1, d–3
  4. Rep. Randy Fine threatened to send a letter to the FCC chairperson Brendan Carr calling for "dramatic action" against the NFL, NBC, and Bad Bunny, including possible fines and broadcast license reviews.
