⚖️ Work With Your Biology, Not Against It


Why midlife weight starts earlier than you think—and what to do about it

© 2025, Mayo Clinic News Network. Distributed by Tribune Content Agency, LLC.

Weight gain is common in women during perimenopause and after menopause, but the physical changes underlying it start much earlier in adulthood. Daniela Hurtado Andrade, M.D., Ph.D., an endocrinologist and obesity expert at Mayo Clinic in Jacksonville, [Florida], explains why it is important to start counteracting those changes before perimenopause arrives.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 1

perimenopause

The time period leading up to menopause when hormone levels start to change

perimenopausia

HR included perimenopause awareness in this year's employee wellness workshops.
underlying

Being the basic cause or reason behind something, often hidden

subyacente

Before fixing the defect, the quality team identified the underlying cause on the assembly line.
counteracting

Taking action to reduce or stop a negative effect

contrarrestar

The maintenance team is counteracting equipment wear by scheduling preventive checks.

"Weight changes are not only characteristic of midlife or menopause. Data have shown that weight gain starts earlier in adulthood," Dr. Hurtado says. "Around age 30, your muscle mass starts decreasing gradually as part of the natural aging process, and this is exacerbated in midlife due to menopause."

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 2

exacerbated

Made a problem worse

agravado / empeorado

The parts shortage was exacerbated by a delay at the supplier's warehouse.

The decreasing muscle mass will lead to a lower metabolism, a risk factor for weight gain, Dr. Hurtado says.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 3

lead to

To result in or cause something

conducir a / resultar en

Skipping the calibration step can lead to inconsistent product quality.
metabolism

The chemical process by which the body converts food into energy

metabolismo

The wellness coach explained how a slower metabolism after age 30 can affect energy levels at work.

Adding to the challenge of achieving a healthy weight before and after menopause are many of the features of modern life, Dr. Hurtado says. For example, an abundance of processed foods high in sugar and fat; technology, such as escalators, elevators and cars, that allows people to move from place to place without walking; and the difficulty of unplugging from devices that keep people connected to work and sedentary for longer hours, with less time spent on physical activity.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 4

unplugging

Disconnecting from work devices or technology in order to rest

desconectarse

Supervisors encourage unplugging from work email after the night shift ends.
sedentary

Involving little physical movement, mostly sitting

sedentario

Office staff are encouraged to take walking breaks to avoid a sedentary workday.

Being aware that weight-related physical changes start earlier than midlife may help people be mindful about the importance of establishing and maintaining a healthy lifestyle, including adequate diet and exercise (at least 150 minutes per week), well ahead of menopause, Dr. Hurtado says. It may help to track what and how much you eat, to understand how much you need to eat to feel satisfied and avoid overeating, she adds.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 5

mindful

Paying careful attention to something; aware

consciente / atento

Operators are trained to be mindful of the safety zones around the machinery.
well ahead of

Much earlier than; with plenty of time before

mucho antes de

The plant manager ordered replacement parts well ahead of the scheduled maintenance.
track

To record and monitor something over time

llevar seguimiento de / rastrear

Supervisors track downtime on each production line every shift.

To mitigate muscle mass loss that occurs with aging, a healthy diet should include enough protein, and exercise should include resistance training and possibly high-intensity interval training [HIIT], Dr. Hurtado says.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 6

resistance training

Exercise that builds muscle strength using weight or resistance

entrenamiento de resistencia/fuerza

The company gym added resistance training equipment for employees to use on their breaks.
high-intensity interval training (HIIT)

Short bursts of intense exercise alternated with rest

entrenamiento en intervalos de alta intensidad

The wellness program offers a 20-minute HIIT class before the morning shift.

While a healthy lifestyle is essential, it may only get someone so far in pursuit of a healthy weight, Dr. Hurtado says.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 7

get someone so far

Idiom: something helps, but has limits and isn't enough alone

tiene un límite / no es suficiente por sí solo

Good intentions will only get you so far; the line also needs proper training and tools.
in pursuit of

While trying to achieve something

en busca de / en la búsqueda de

In pursuit of zero defects, the team redesigned the entire inspection process.

"We know that weight-loss interventions are also associated with muscle mass loss, which can have a significant impact on the weight-loss outcomes," Dr. Hurtado says. "One of the reasons why we hit a plateau when we are losing weight is because we are losing muscle mass and our metabolism slows down."

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 8

outcomes

The results of an action or process

resultados

The safety committee reviews quarterly outcomes from every audit.
hit a plateau

To reach a point where progress stops

llegar a un estancamiento

Productivity hit a plateau after the initial improvements from the new software.

The body is genetically programmed to survive, so losing weight is perceived as negative and our bodies will try to fight it. Some people can sustain low-calorie diets for a period of time and lose weight, but eventually they may start feeling hungry, eating more and gaining weight.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 9

perceived as

Understood or seen in a particular way

percibido como

Overtime is often perceived as a sign of poor planning rather than dedication.
sustain

To keep something going over a long period

sostener / mantener

It's hard to sustain peak output on the line without proper breaks and rotation.

"As soon as some people even think about losing weight or restricting their calories, their brain overrides those thoughts: 'No, you're actually hungry, so you're going to eat as much as you need to make sure that you do not lose weight,'" Dr. Hurtado says. "There is a proportion of people in whom diet and exercise alone will work, but for the majority, they will not due to the complex regulation of energy balance."

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 10

overrides

To take control over and cancel another decision or instinct

anula / tiene prioridad sobre

Safety protocol always overrides the production schedule.
regulation of energy balance

The body's control system for managing energy intake and use

regulación del equilibrio energético

Just like the body's energy balance, the plant needs careful regulation of power use during peak hours.

Many people who live with overweight or obesity will need a tool such as a medication, an endoscopic bariatric procedure or bariatric surgery to help them achieve sustained weight loss, Dr. Hurtado says. In addition to nutritional and behavioral support, they also may benefit from physical therapy or occupational therapy to help them find ways to become physically active, she adds.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 11

endoscopic bariatric procedure

A minimally invasive medical procedure that helps with weight loss

procedimiento bariátrico endoscópico

The company's health insurance now covers consultations for an endoscopic bariatric procedure.
benefit from

To receive an advantage or improvement from something

beneficiarse de

New hires benefit from a structured onboarding program during their first month.

"Every visit with a doctor is an opportunity, especially in these times when overweight and obesity are so highly prevalent," she says. "We want to prevent rather than treat something that may be more difficult to treat for several reasons. Lifestyle interventions are the core of all weight management interventions. While we need to understand that there is no magic pill, there are tools that can facilitate adhering to lifestyle changes."

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 12

prevalent

Common or widespread at a particular time

prevalente / común

Repetitive strain injuries are prevalent among workers on the packaging line.
the core of

The most essential or central part of something

el núcleo de / la base de

Clear communication is the core of every successful shift handover.
facilitate

To make a process easier

facilitar

The new software facilitates tracking inventory across all three warehouses.

Dr. Hurtado emphasizes that obesity is a disease. "Starting the conversation about weight management with that message may help people have a different perspective: 'OK, so they are talking about something that is not my fault, but actually has a biological basis,'" Dr. Hurtado says. "Realizing that weight problems are a disease and not a character flaw can help them think about it differently and could be very impactful on the outcomes of weight loss interventions."

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 13

character flaw

A personal weakness or fault, often blamed on someone's personality

defecto de carácter

Missing a deadline because of a supply issue shouldn't be treated as a character flaw.
impactful

Having a strong effect or influence

impactante / de gran impacto

The new safety training was one of the most impactful changes this year.

💬 Discussion Questions

  • How does your workplace support employees in building healthy habits, such as exercise or nutrition, during busy shifts?
  • Dr. Hurtado says obesity is a disease, not a character flaw. How might changing the way we talk about health issues affect how coworkers support each other?
  • What "tools" (training, equipment, schedule changes) has your company used to prevent problems rather than just fix them after they happen?

✍️ Self-Practice

  1. Fill in the blank: Skipping regular maintenance checks can ______ ______ costly equipment failures. (use a phrasal expression from Paragraph 3)
  2. True or False: According to Dr. Hurtado, diet and exercise alone are enough for most people to achieve permanent weight loss.
  3. Short answer: In your own words, explain what it means for something to be "counterproductive" at work. Give an example from your own job.
  4. Workplace connection: Think of a process at your company that has "hit a plateau." What changes might help move past it?
Reference Answers
  1. ...can lead to costly equipment failures.
  2. False. Dr. Hurtado says that while diet and exercise alone work for some people, for the majority they are not enough due to the complex regulation of energy balance.
  3. Open-ended — students should explain that "counterproductive" means an action has the opposite effect of what was intended, and give a real workplace example (e.g., rushing a process and creating more errors).
  4. Open-ended — students should identify a real process and suggest a concrete change (new training, new equipment, a different schedule, etc.).
