🏜️ Hotel in the High Desert

Where you feel like you are camping on Mars

by Deborah Netburn / © 2025, Los Angeles Times.
Distributed by Tribune Content Agency, LLC.

Architectural designer Ben Uyeda was stretched out poolside in a rare moment of repose at Reset, his new hotel in 29 Palms, California.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 1

stretched out

Lying down in a relaxed, extended position

tumbado, relajado

During his lunch break, the maintenance supervisor was stretched out on the break room bench.
repose

A state of rest or calm

descanso, reposo

After the night shift, the workers finally had a moment of repose in the break room.

"This is the best time to watch the mountains," he said, looking south toward the direction of Joshua Tree National Park. "Right now, they look really jagged, but they'll turn soft and orange as the sun sets."

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 2

jagged

Having a rough or pointed edge or surface

dentado, irregular

Operators must handle the sheet metal carefully because the freshly cut edges are jagged.

Sitting next to him were his collaborators Emi Kitawaki and Jen Whitaker of the design firm Gry Space who created the look and tone of Reset's spare, earthy interiors. Sharing a cold cucumber salad from the hotel's in-house restaurant, they gazed across the spacious pool area with its wide platform loungers and stucco walls that perfectly matched the color of the dusty landscape beyond.

"The sky is the show," Kitawaki said, looking up.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 3-4

spare

Plain or basic, using nothing unnecessary

sencillo, austero

The new production layout has a spare design, with only the essential equipment on the floor.
gazed

Looked steadily and for a long time at something

miraron fijamente

The visiting client gazed at the assembly line, impressed by how organized it was.
stucco

A type of plaster used to coat walls

estuco, revestimiento de yeso

The exterior of the new warehouse office was finished with a smooth stucco coating.

Made for stargazing

Reset is a new kind of hotel for the high desert. Modern. Minimal. Modular. While the pool area with its hot tub, sauna and thoughtful landscaping is pure desert luxury, the rest of the hotel consists of 65 rectangular-shaped, free-standing rooms laid out in neat rows. Each sparsely furnished room is outfitted with carefully selected amenities that suggest a highly curated camping trip—a stylish solar lantern, in-room pour-over coffee, an outdoor fire pit and, most importantly, a sturdy cushion on the private outdoor patio for stargazing.

"The best view here is always up," Uyeda said.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 5-6

modular

Made of standard units or sections that can be combined

modular, compuesto de módulos

Switching to a modular assembly system allowed the plant to reconfigure the line in under a day.
free-standing

Not attached to or supported by anything else

independiente, autónomo

Each free-standing workstation can be moved without disconnecting the others.
sparsely

Existing only in small amounts; thinly

escasamente

The pilot line was sparsely staffed while the company tested the new process.
outfitted

Equipped or supplied with necessary items

equipado, dotado

Every operator's station was outfitted with the correct safety gear before the shift began.
curated

Carefully chosen, organized and presented

cuidadosamente seleccionado

The supplier sent a curated selection of samples that matched our quality specifications.

Science-fiction style

The rooms are not technically built from shipping containers, but with their boxy shape, corrugated silver sides and narrow dimensions, they sure look like it. Walking along the hotel's concrete paths, the overall effect is far more sci-fi than Old West.

The hotel's designers say the evocation of space travel was deliberate.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 7-8

boxy

Square or rectangular in shape, like a box

rectangular, con forma de caja

The old boxy machine was replaced with a more compact, streamlined model.
corrugated

Shaped into folds or waves to add strength

ondulado, corrugado

The warehouse roof is made of corrugated steel panels for extra durability.
evocation

Something that produces a strong feeling or memory

evocación

The new showroom design is an evocation of the company's original 1980s workshop.

"When we came onto the project, the first thing Ben mentioned was what would humans do if we built a community on Mars," Kitawaki said. "Everything is designed from a more mission, utilitarian standpoint: What is actually needed?"

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 9

came onto (a project)

Joined or started working on something

se sumó al, se unió al

The new quality engineer came onto the project just as the certification audit began.
utilitarian

Designed to be practical and useful rather than decorative

utilitario, práctico

The factory floor has a utilitarian layout, built purely for efficiency.

Hotels and affordable housing

A handful of new hotels opened in the Joshua Tree area, as tourism has soared over the last several years. But Reset is the first hotel to be built from the ground up in the area in 15 years.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 10

a handful of

A small number of something

unos cuantos, un puñado de

Only a handful of suppliers can meet our new tolerance requirements.
soared

Rose quickly and to a high level

se disparó, aumentó rápidamente

Demand for the new product line soared after the trade show.
from the ground up

Starting from the very beginning, with nothing already built

desde cero

The new facility was designed from the ground up to meet export compliance standards.

Uyeda, who has more than 1.6 million subscribers on his YouTube channel HomeMade Modern, got involved with the project after building a shipping container house in Joshua Tree and releasing a docuseries detailing the process in 2020. A former professor of architecture at Northeastern and Cornell universities, he'd noticed that as home prices skyrocketed there was a corresponding increase of interest in potentially more affordable construction like tiny homes, earthships and container homes.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 11

got involved with

Became a participant in something

se involucró en

The maintenance team got involved with the redesign after noticing repeated equipment failures.
skyrocketed

Increased very quickly and by a large amount

se dispararon

Raw material costs skyrocketed last quarter, forcing us to adjust our pricing.
earthships

Self-sufficient homes built from recycled and natural materials

ecoconstrucciones, casas autosuficientes

Some clients now ask if our packaging materials could be repurposed the way earthships reuse waste materials.

Uyeda began having conversations with his now business partner Adam Wininger about how the rise of Airbnb was converting desperately needed housing in the desert to vacation rentals. They wondered if building modular structures on land that was already zoned for commercial use might be one solution to increasing lodging units without impacting local housing supply. While the process of building modular is not cheaper than a regular build, it is faster, especially in a remote desert location.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 12

desperately needed

Urgently required

urgentemente necesario

The plant received desperately needed replacement parts just before the deadline.
zoned for

Officially designated for a specific type of use

zonificado para

The empty lot next to the warehouse is zoned for industrial expansion.
impacting

Having a noticeable effect on something

afectando

The supplier delay ended up impacting three separate production lines.

Feeling connected

Construction began in 2023. Room modules were made in a factory outside of Ontario, Canada, at the same time foundation work was occurring in 29 Palms. Uyeda promoted the hotel on his social media feeds by releasing DIY videos demonstrating how he and his small team hand-built 500 pieces of furniture, including fold-out desks, couches, daybeds and a vase made out of rock.

"When you come here, it feels personal," Kitawaki said. "You're connected to the items you see in the hotel, because you saw how they were made."

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 13-14

foundation work

Construction of the base structure that supports a building

trabajo de cimentación

Foundation work on the new warehouse extension will take about three weeks.
hand-built

Made by hand rather than by machine

fabricado a mano

The prototype was hand-built by the engineering team before mass production began.
fold-out

Able to be unfolded or extended for use

plegable

Each inspection station has a fold-out table to save floor space.

When I first arrived at Reset, I was taken aback by the starkness of the space. But it didn't take long for the hotel's minimalism and clean lines to grow on me. Whether I was in bed, at the pool or lying on my private patio, the thoughtfully designed spaces always encouraged me to look out and up.

📚 Vocabulary - Paragraph 15

taken aback

To be very surprised about something

sorprendido/a

The floor supervisor was taken aback by how quickly the new team adapted to the machines.
starkness

The quality of being plain, bare or severe

austeridad, lo desnudo

Visitors are often surprised by the starkness of a fully automated production line.
grow on me

To gradually become more likable or appealing over time

irle gustando a alguien poco a poco

The new shift schedule was unpopular at first, but it grew on the workers after a month.

💬 Discussion Questions

  • Would modular construction methods, like prefabricated room units built in a factory, work well in your industry? Why or why not?
  • The hotel's designers focused on "what is actually needed" from a utilitarian standpoint. How do you apply this kind of thinking when designing a process or a product on your production line?
  • Ben Uyeda used social media and DIY videos to build trust with future guests by showing how things were made. How could sharing your company's manufacturing process build trust with clients?

✍️ Self-Practice

1. Fill in the blank with the correct word: jagged, modular, corrugated, skyrocketed

a) The mountains looked in the afternoon light.

b) The hotel rooms have a silver exterior that looks like a shipping container.

c) Home prices in the area over the past several years.

d) Building the hotel with a design allowed rooms to be built off-site.

2. True or False

a) Reset was the first hotel built from the ground up in the Joshua Tree area in 15 years. ____

b) The hotel's rooms are actually made from real shipping containers. ____

c) Room modules were manufactured in a factory outside of Ontario, Canada. ____

d) Ben Uyeda had no previous experience with modular or container construction before Reset. ____

3. Short Answer

a) Why did Uyeda and his business partner consider building on land already zoned for commercial use?

b) According to Kitawaki, why does the hotel feel "personal" to guests?

4. Workplace Connection

Think about a process in your own workplace that was redesigned "from the ground up." What made the change necessary, and was the new approach faster, cheaper, or both?

Reference Answers
  1. a) jagged   b) corrugated   c) skyrocketed   d) modular
  2. a) True   b) False (they only look like shipping containers)   c) True   d) False (he had previously built a shipping container house and made a docuseries about it)
  3. a) They wanted to increase lodging units without impacting local housing supply, since Airbnb rentals were converting desperately needed housing into vacation rentals.   b) Because guests can see how the furniture and spaces were made, which creates a personal connection to the items around them.
